The idea

Not one giant app. One digital ecosystem.

Each layer solves a different job: Discover Pipa attracts and guides; business pages convert; menus simplify decisions; WhatsApp reduces friction; the app drives retention; automation connects operations; AI arrives when data and processes are reliable.

The goal is not digitalization for its own sake. It is to connect the visitor experience more effectively with the group's businesses and real operations.

Must validate in reality

On-site in Pipa

  • Who owns each WhatsApp and who responds
  • Reservation workflow and staff responsibilities
  • Actual availability and capacity logic
  • Deposits, no-show, refunds and payment ownership
  • Current prices and seasonal rules
  • Real opening times and exceptions
  • Current venue media and access information
  • Booking/PMS/ticketing software already in use
  • Which automations truly reduce work for staff

Bottom line

Start with the customer journey. Add operational complexity only when the business is ready.

The project can grow from a strong destination + business platform into a complete digital ecosystem without throwing away the work already built. Every new layer — app, loyalty, reservations, automation, payments or AI — should plug into the same structured data and customer journey.
